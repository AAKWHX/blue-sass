# Pricing review — 28 September 2026

Approved by owner: EUR for the Dutch market; 10% discount on project implementation only. Subscriptions and external services excluded. No expiry date or scarcity claim was authorised.

Blue Sass indicative scopes and rates are defined in `lib/website-packages.ts` and `lib/pricing.ts`. They are our estimates, not market averages or binding quotes. Internal labour costs were not supplied: profitability is not verified and every final proposal needs a delivery-cost review. Complex platforms begin with an MVP, not an unlimited enterprise build. Existing contracts and saved request budgets are not repriced.

## Primary references checked

- FusionStudios publishes landing pages €500–1,000, websites €2,000–3,500 and shops €3,500–7,500, excluding VAT: https://www.fusionstudios.nl/wat-kost-een-website-laten-maken/
- Baken Studio advertises starter sites from €695 and growth sites €1,095; evidence that lower-scope Dutch offers exist: https://bakenstudio.nl/wat-kost-een-website-laten-maken
- Stripe Netherlands lists standard EEA cards at 1.5% + €0.25 and iDEAL at €0.29 per payment: https://stripe.com/nl/pricing
- Shopify platform fees are separate from agency implementation, subject to billing interval and changing terms: https://www.shopify.com/nl/prijzen

These sources are a pricing sanity check, not copied specifications. Review platform fees and rate assumptions before providing a binding offer. Promotion is computed by the same implementation estimator on client and server; optional recurring services never enter its subtotal. Estimates are rounded to whole euros consistently for the existing integer budget schema.
